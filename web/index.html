Thin gravitational lenses in units of the Einstein radius θE. The models are a point mass and the softened isothermal ellipsoid of Keeton & Kochanek (1998), as given in Keeton (2001), plus an external shear. Images of the source center are found by mapping triangles of a polar grid in the sky through the lens equation and refining each candidate with Newton's method; the extended source is drawn by tracing each sky pixel back to the source plane. Time delays assume θE = 1″, a lens at z = 0.5 and a source at z = 2 with the Planck 2018 cosmology, for which one unit of arrival time is 86 days; a point mass with this Einstein radius has 2.5 × 1011 M☉, and a singular isothermal sphere a velocity dispersion of 234 km s−1.
